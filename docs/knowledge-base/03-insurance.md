# Insurance and Coverage

MolarAI Dental Studio is an independent, out-of-network clinic and does not promise direct billing with any insurer. Patients may ask the front desk for an itemized receipt to submit to their dental plan. Reimbursement depends on the patient's individual plan and is determined by the insurer.

Before a planned procedure, the clinic can provide an estimate based on the proposed treatment. An estimate is not a guarantee of insurance payment or final coverage. Patients should contact their insurer with coverage questions.
