# Clinic Hours

MolarAI Dental Studio is a fictional neighborhood dental clinic at 42 Juniper Commons, Portland, OR 97205. The clinic is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. All times are Pacific Time.
