# Dental Urgent and Emergency Care

During opening hours, call (503) 555-0142 for a painful tooth, a lost filling, or a chipped tooth. The clinic reserves limited same-day evaluation slots when available; an emergency evaluation is listed at $125 before treatment. Calling does not guarantee an immediate appointment.

For uncontrolled bleeding, serious facial injury, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or another life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. MolarAI Dental Studio is not an overnight service and cannot provide emergency care while closed. This information is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
