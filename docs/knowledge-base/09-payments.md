# Payment Information

Payment is due at the time of service unless the clinic has agreed to another arrangement in writing. MolarAI Dental Studio accepts major credit and debit cards, cash, and health savings account cards. The clinic does not currently offer in-house financing.

Because the clinic is out of network, patients pay the clinic directly and may submit an itemized receipt to their insurer. A written treatment estimate is available before planned care. Contact the front desk at (503) 555-0142 with billing questions.
