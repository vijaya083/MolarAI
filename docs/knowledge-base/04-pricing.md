# Example Pricing

These fictional self-pay prices are in U.S. dollars and are effective for this sample clinic knowledge base: new-patient comprehensive exam, $95; routine cleaning, $110; bitewing X-rays when needed, $35; tooth-colored filling, from $185; and emergency evaluation during clinic hours, $125.

Prices are estimates, may change after an examination, and do not include additional treatment unless stated. The front desk confirms the expected patient portion before scheduled treatment whenever practical. Insurance reimbursements, if any, are handled by the patient with their plan.
