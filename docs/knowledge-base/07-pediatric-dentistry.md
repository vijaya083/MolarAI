# Pediatric Dentistry

MolarAI Dental Studio welcomes children age 5 and older for preventive exams and cleanings. A parent or legal guardian must attend and provide consent for treatment. The team uses age-appropriate explanations and can discuss a gradual first visit for a child who is nervous.

The standard new-patient exam price is $95 and a routine cleaning is $110; additional services are discussed with the guardian before proceeding. The clinic does not offer pediatric hospital dentistry or sedation services. For a child younger than 5, the front desk can suggest asking a pediatric dental specialist for guidance.
