# Dental Services

MolarAI Dental Studio offers preventive checkups and cleanings, dental X-rays when clinically appropriate, fillings, crowns, root canal treatment, simple tooth extractions, and routine gum care. Cosmetic consultations for whitening and veneers are available by appointment.

The clinic does not perform hospital surgery or provide orthodontic treatment. A dentist first examines each patient and discusses suitable options, expected costs, and any referral needs before treatment begins.
