# Information for New Patients

New patients should arrive 15 minutes before their first appointment and bring a photo ID, a list of current medications and allergies, and any recent dental X-rays they can access. Please bring dental-plan details if you would like an itemized receipt for possible reimbursement; MolarAI Dental Studio is out of network and does not guarantee insurance coverage.

A first comprehensive exam is scheduled for about 60 minutes and is listed at $95 before any additional services. The dentist reviews health history, examines the mouth, discusses findings, and explains proposed next steps. Call (503) 555-0142 to ask about accessibility needs or records transfer.
