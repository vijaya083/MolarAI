# Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

Patients should call (503) 555-0142 at least 24 hours before an appointment to cancel or reschedule. Appointments changed with less than 24 hours' notice or missed without notice may incur a $35 missed-appointment fee. The fee is not charged for a first-time occurrence or when the office manager waives it for an emergency or other reasonable circumstance.

Late arrival may shorten the visit or require rescheduling so the clinic can stay on schedule. The front desk will explain any fee before applying it.
